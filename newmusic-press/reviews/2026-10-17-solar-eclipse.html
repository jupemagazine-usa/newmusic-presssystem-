Quick answer

"Solar Eclipse" by Drake and Don Toliver is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs for the chart dated October 17, 2026 — a debut week at the top that ends, for one week, the record run of "I Just Might," and is also No. 1 the same week on Hot R&B Songs. The headline metadata finding: this record carries thirteen credited writers drawn from three different songs, and the one man who produced, programmed, mixed, and recorded it is credited in full — while no publisher, no studio, and no video crew member is named anywhere public.

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Read the writer list and you read three records at once. "Solar Eclipse" interpolates "Choosin' Texas" — the country record that sits at No. 2 on the Hot 100 in this same chart week — so its four writers (Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Luke Dick, Joybeth Taylor) are credited here. It also samples "Get Throwed," the 2005 Houston record by Bun B with Pimp C, Z-Ro, Jeezy, and Jay-Z, so that record's writers are credited here too. Add Drake, Don Toliver, and producer Noel Cadastre, and one 3-minute-38-second recording carries thirteen writer credits across country, Houston rap, and Toronto.

The making is the opposite shape: one man. The official audio feed credits Noel Cadastre as producer, programmer, mixing engineer, and recording engineer — four departments in one name, fully published. The recording line reads "℗ 2026 OVO, under exclusive license to Republic Records" — the artist's own house holds the ℗ line and licenses it to the major, the reverse of Monday's and last Wednesday's label-held lines.

Then the chain goes dark. No publisher for any of the thirteen writers is published anywhere found; no studio is named; the official video's crew is credited nowhere. And the loudest money claim about this record — a reported, unconfirmed claim that Drake waived his own publishing share so the interpolated writers would take the publishing — comes from a livestream, not from any party to the record. Who collects the writing money on a thirteen-writer No. 1 is, in public, [needs confirmation].
