Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Every season this series has traced chains that run through partners, licensees, and outside houses. This one barely leaves the building. "God I'm Just Grateful" was written by five writers anchored by Elevation Church's own pastor and worship team, produced by six producers from the same camp, recorded live at Elevation Church in Charlotte, North Carolina, in September 2025, and released on Elevation Worship Records — the church's own label, independently — which also provided the record to YouTube under its own name. Even the publishing is published, house by house: Elevation Worship Publishing leads a named line of six publishers on the official resources page, with a CCLI number any church can license against.

The one true partner line is the guest: Chandler Moore — co-writer, co-producer, featured voice — whose side of the publishing (Maverick City Publishing) stands in the same published line. One house, one named partner, and a paper trail cleaner than records with a major behind them usually manage. What the chain does not publish is the machinery end: no distributor, no video crew, no budget — and the chart itself spent the season behind a gate, readable free only in the moment before its paywall settled.
