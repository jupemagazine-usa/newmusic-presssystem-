Quick answer

"This Time" by Kirk Franklin featuring GloRilla is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Gospel Songs for the chart dated October 24, 2026 — a third week on top. The delta in one line: the count climbed in silence — no free report marked weeks two or three — the one metadata defect the first review flagged is still unfixed for a third week, and the album this single leads is now fully public: a double album, out in five days, on the same license line.

Q: What is the No. 1 song on Hot Gospel Songs this week? A: "This Time" by Kirk Franklin featuring GloRilla, No. 1 on the chart dated October 24, 2026, in its third week at No. 1.

Q: How is the third week verified, if no report covers it? A: From Billboard's own chart page for Oct. 24, 2026: its No. 1 card, captured in the live browser before the page's Pro gate rendered, reads "This Time" with LW 1 and PEAK 1. The LW 1 covers the Oct. 17 week; the debut week was verified by four independent reports. The full method is in this review's verification log.

Q: Who wrote "This Time"? A: Kirk Franklin, Gloria Woods, Maxwell Stark, and Ronald Hill — with Carlos D. Wilson, Louis Wilson, and Ricardo Wilson, the writers of Mandrill's "Children of the Sun," which the record interpolates.

Q: What label released "This Time"? A: Fo Yo Soul Recordings and TRIBL Music Group, under exclusive license to Uptown Records, a division of UMG Recordings, Inc. — the same line that carries the double album I Am What I Am (Oct. 30, 2026).

Q: Who worked on "This Time" behind the scenes? A: Producers Maxwell Stark and co-producer Ronald Hill; A&R from Hill and Edward "Gunnz" Gantt; five named engineers including immersive engineer Jacob "Biz" Morris; and four named background vocalists — the roll call is above. The publishers and the choir's name are credited nowhere public found.

Q: What is still wrong with the record's own listing? A: On YouTube's auto-generated listing, the ℗ line still drops Fo Yo Soul Recordings — the artist's own label — for a third week, while Apple and Spotify carry the full line. Flagged in Pass A.
