Quick answer

"Choosin' Texas" by Ella Langley is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Country Songs for the chart dated November 7, 2026 — its 47th week at the top of that chart, three weeks from tying the longest run in the chart's history. The headline finding this week is again a vertical one: the record itself did not move — same four writers, same producers, same named Nashville room, same imprint-first recording line — while the ground around it kept shifting: the song holds No. 1 on the Hot 100 for a 26th week, past half a calendar year on top; it takes back the Digital Song Sales crown for a 20th week; its album's tour closes; the songwriter-chart run ends; the Country Airplay crown passes outside its album — and its publishing, mastering, and collection end remains published nowhere, for the fourth Tuesday review in a row.

What changed — the delta

The count. 46 weeks became 47. At 47, the run stands alone in second place in the chart's history, behind only Bebe Rexha and Florida Georgia Line's "Meant To Be" (50 weeks, 2017–2018) — three more weeks tie that record, a fourth breaks it.
