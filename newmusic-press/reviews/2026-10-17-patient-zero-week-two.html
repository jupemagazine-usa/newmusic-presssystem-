Quick answer

"Patient Zero" by Taylor Swift is No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 for the chart dated October 17, 2026 — a second consecutive week at the top, after debuting at No. 1 the week before as her 16th career leader. The headline metadata finding for week two: the record itself did not change — title, credits, label, and the ℗ line read exactly as they did at debut — but its public numbers moved hard (streams down by half, held up by radio growth and physical CD sales counted this week), and one new name appeared on the chain: the auto-generated YouTube audio listing now states the recording was provided to YouTube by Universal Music Group, a delivery line that was not in last week's public record.

What changed — the delta

The hold. Week two is the test a debut cannot pass on its own. The song held No. 1 while its streaming fell 50% — the balance of the Hot 100, not any single lane, carried it: radio rose on its component chart, digital sales held their top spot, and physical sales arrived in force.

The numbers (tracking week October 2–8, 2026, as reported): - Streams: 17.4M U.S. official streams, down 50% from 34.5M in the debut week - Radio: 26.1M audience impressions, down 9% from 28.5M — and up on the component chart, Radio Songs No. 16 → No. 15 - Sales: 8,000 downloads, plus 134,000 CD copies reported sold in the U.S. from October 2 to 8, counted in this week's sum — the CD singles' first week of availability, per the reports - Component charts: Streaming Songs No. 1 → No. 2 · Radio Songs No. 16 → No. 15 · Digital Song Sales No. 1 for a second week - Career total: the hold gives the artist her 51st career week at No. 1 on the Hot 100, the fifth-highest total in the chart's history as reported (behind Mariah Carey 101, Rihanna 60, the Beatles 59, Drake 58 — one report's club list reads Drake at 57; that outside total is in conflict and is not printed as fact) - Sources: https://en.playback.gr/Second-week-at-No.-1-for-Taylor-Swift%27s-Patient-Zero-on-the-Billboard-Hot-100/ · https://thatgrapejuice.net/2026/10/patient-zero-taylor-swift-joins-rihanna-mariah-carey-as-the-only-women-in-hot-100-history-with-over-50-career-weeks-at-1/ · http://erius-mag.blogspot.com/2026/10/taylor-swifts-patient-zero-holds-steady.html

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Week two changes one line of the chain and leaves the rest standing.

The new line: YouTube's auto-generated audio listing for "Patient Zero" states "Provided to YouTube by Universal Music Group", credits the composers and engineers in full, and carries the same ℗ 2026 Taylor Swift line. So on that platform, the delivery hand is now named in public — Universal Music Group put the recording on YouTube — while the recording itself still stands in the artist's own name, and Republic Records remains the label of release. Whether Universal Music Group is the distributor for the release as a whole is not stated in any source checked: that stays [needs confirmation], and the YouTube line is claimed for YouTube only. - Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-uIp-WuD60 (auto-generated listing, "Patient Zero · Taylor Swift", ℗ 2026 Taylor Swift, "Provided to YouTube by Universal Music Group", full credit block)

Everything else in the chain reads as it did at debut: written and produced by the artist with Max Martin and Shellback, finished by named engineers, filmed by a fully credited crew under Merman & Taylor Swift Productions, released through Republic while the ℗ line stays with the artist — and the publishing side, who collects the writing money, still published nowhere.
