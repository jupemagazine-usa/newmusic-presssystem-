"This Time" by Kirk Franklin featuring GloRilla is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Gospel Songs for the chart dated October 10, 2026, debuting at the top — reported as Franklin's record-extending 10th No. 1 on that chart and GloRilla's second. The headline metadata finding: this is the most completely credited record of the week on its audio side — and on one platform, the artist's own label name is simply missing from the recording line.

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

The recording line reads "℗ 2026 Fo Yo Soul Recordings/TRIBL Music Group, LLC, under exclusive license to Uptown Records, a division of UMG Recordings, Inc." — the same line on Apple and Spotify. Reporting on the album cycle names the structure behind it: the artist's own Fo Yo Soul and TRIBL Music Group in partnership with Uptown. So the top of this chain is held by the artist's house and its gospel partner, and the major division works under their license — ownership at home, reach rented.

The finding inside the finding: YouTube's auto-generated listing for the same recording omits Fo Yo Soul Recordings from the line entirely. The owner's own name — the first name on the chain — is the one the platform dropped. That is the vertical story and the metadata story in a single line, and it is documented both ways in Pass A below. - Sources: Spotify single listing; Apple album listing; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UymzvIme3bU (auto-generated listing) · https://www.udiscovermusic.com/news/kirk-franklin-this-time/
