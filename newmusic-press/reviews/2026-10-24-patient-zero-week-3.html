Quick answer

"Patient Zero" by Taylor Swift is No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 for the chart dated October 24, 2026 — a third consecutive week at the top, taking her career total to 52 weeks at No. 1 on the chart. The headline metadata finding for week three: the record's chain did not move at all — same writers, same producers, same engineers, same artist-held ℗ line — but the week was won in a different lane than the first two: sales. Downloads rose 246% and physical copies rose 12% on the arrival of the song's acoustic and piano versions for digital download and streaming and its 7-inch vinyl in the artist's webstore, while streaming fell for a second straight week — and the new versions arrived with no public credit block of their own.

What changed — the delta

The hold, third form. Week one was a debut. Week two was a hold carried by balance. Week three is a hold carried by sales: the song stays at No. 1 while its streaming falls again, because the sales lanes — the lanes a storefront and a release calendar control — rose hard in the same tracking week.

The numbers (tracking week October 9–15, 2026, as reported): - Streams: 12.8M U.S. official streams, down 27% from 17.4M in week two - Radio: 28.2M audience impressions, up 8% from 26.1M — and the song holds its No. 15 high on Radio Songs - Downloads: 27,000 sold, up 246% from 8,000 - Physical: 151,000 copies sold, up 12% from 134,000 - Component charts: Streaming Songs No. 2 → No. 3 · Radio Songs No. 15, holding its high · Digital Song Sales No. 1 for a third week - Career total: the third week gives the artist her 52nd career week at No. 1 on the Hot 100 — the equivalent of a full year on top, one of five acts at that mark as reported (Mariah Carey 101, Rihanna 60, the Beatles 59, Drake 58, Taylor Swift 52) - Sources: https://www.europesays.com/ie/742228/ · https://en.playback.gr/Taylor-Swift's-Patient-Zero-Remains-at-No.-1-on-Billboard-Hot-100-for-Third-Week/

What drove the sales. The chart report names the mechanism plainly: boosting the song's totals in the tracking week were the arrivals of its acoustic and piano versions, for digital download and on streaming services, and its 7-inch vinyl version sold in the artist's webstore. The versions are not new creations of week three — acoustic and piano editions were among the collector's CD variants sold at launch in September — but their arrival in the digital and streaming lanes is a week-three event, and the report credits it with the week's shape. Note the chart rule the report restates: digital single sales from direct-to-consumer sites are excluded from the Hot 100 sum; the physical copies count where they qualify. - Source: https://www.europesays.com/ie/742228/

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Week three changes no line of the chain — and that is the story. The record that held No. 1 for a third week is, credit for credit and line for line, the record that debuted there: written and produced by the artist with Max Martin and Shellback, finished by the same named engineers, filmed by a fully credited crew under Merman & Taylor Swift Productions, released through Republic Records while the ℗ line stays in the artist's own name, with Universal Music Group named in public only as the provider to YouTube. What changed is which lane of that chain did the week's work: not a new credit, not a new company — new versions of the same recording, sold through the artist's own storefront and the download stores, plus radio holding at its peak position.

One line deserves to be said plainly, because it is this series' whole reason for existing: the versions that carried week three — the acoustic and the piano recordings — have no public credits. The original recording's makers are named everywhere. The versions' performers and engineers are named nowhere public found. The chain held; the newest links in it are the least documented. - Sources: https://www.europesays.com/ie/742228/ · https://www.qobuz.com/gb-en/album/the-life-of-a-showgirl-the-encore-taylor-swift/wzcuiwbkbeqh5 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-uIp-WuD60
