Quick answer

"BbY WOW" by Karol G with Judeline and Rusowsky is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Latin Songs for the chart dated November 7, 2026 — an eleventh week on top, the second-longest command on that chart in 2026. The delta in one line: the crown that left last week came back — the record reclaimed Latin Airplay and, with it, the lead artist swept that chart's top three with songs from one album for the first time in its 32-year history — while the chain behind the record, and every gap in it, did not move at all.

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Vertical headline: the chain did not move in paper — the same six writers, the same five producers, the same four engineers, and the same artist-held recording line, Bichota Records LLC under exclusive license to Interscope Records — while the radio end of the chain, the one end that moved away last week, came back and made history: the Latin Airplay crown returned for a third week (11.1 million impressions, reported) as the lead artist swept that chart's Nos. 1, 2 and 3 with songs from this one album, a first in that chart's 32 years, and a named executive of the major's Miami house spoke for the label in public for the first time in this series — while the publishing end of the chain, including the sampled riddim creator's official writer status, remains published nowhere, for an eleventh week.

Eleven weeks in, the shape of this record's chain is still the finding — and this week it gained a public face at exactly one joint. The work at the top of the chain is held close: the lead artist's own company holds both recording lines and licenses the major for reach; the three billed artists wrote and produced the record beside two outside producers who are also writers. The radio system the major runs then did something no album has done on that chart in 32 years — it carried three songs from this one album into the top three at once, with this record back on top — and, for the first time in this series, the major's house spoke in its own name in the coverage, through its Miami general manager. And the two ends of the chain that were dark at week 1 are dark at week 11: no publisher, rights home, administrator, or ISWC is public anywhere found, and the official film of this record's biggest stage moment still carries no crew credit. The record travels the world, and now its label speaks. The paper still stays home, unpublished.
