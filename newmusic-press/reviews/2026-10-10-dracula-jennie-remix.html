Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

The ℗ line reads "℗ 2026 Columbia Records, a Division of Sony Music Entertainment, under exclusive license from Tame Impala" — the major's name is on the line, but the line itself says the recording is licensed in from the artist, not owned outright. And the making of this record is the most self-contained chain of the week: Kevin Parker wrote the song (with Sarah Aarons), produced it alone, and on the remix he is also a mix engineer and a recording engineer — one person standing in the writing room, behind the board, and at the mix desk. Jennie enters the chain as a working partner, credited not only as vocalist but as songwriter and remixer. What the chain rents is the major's reach — distribution and radio — and a marketing campaign built with the label (see §13).

The end of the chain is dark: no publisher, rights home, administrator, or ISWC for the remix is public anywhere found — [needs confirmation] across the whole rights side. - Sources: Apple Music single listing (℗ line) · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dracula_(Tame_Impala_song) · https://www.kpopstarz.com/articles/323372/20260926/blackpinks-jennie-tame-impala-score-first-radio-songs-no-1-dracula.htm
