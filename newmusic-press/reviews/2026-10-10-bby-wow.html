Quick answer

"BbY WOW" by Karol G with Judeline and Rusowsky is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Latin Songs for the chart dated October 10, 2026 — the Spanish collaborators' first No. 1 on that chart beside the artist's eleventh. The headline metadata finding: even the song's own name will not sit still — the platforms style it "BbY WOW" and bill one of its makers in lowercase, while the reference pages style it "Bby Wow" — and nobody can agree in public on how many weeks it has been No. 1.

Vertical Integration — Who Owns the Chain

The headline angle of this review, from verified lines only.

Both recording lines read "© 2026 Bichota Records LLC, under exclusive license to Interscope Records / ℗ 2026 Bichota Records LLC, under exclusive license to Interscope Records." The structure is printed on the record itself: the artist's own company holds the work, and the major works under a license from her house. The three billed artists also stand inside the making — all three are credited producers alongside Tainy and Omer Fedi — so the front of the chain (writing room, production desk, recording line) sits with the artists and the artist's company, and what is rented is the major's distribution and reach.
