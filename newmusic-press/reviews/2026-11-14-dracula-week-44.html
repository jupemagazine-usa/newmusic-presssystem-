Quick answer

"Dracula" (remix) by Tame Impala and Jennie is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Dance/Electronic Songs for the chart dated November 14, 2026 — a 44th total week on top. The delta in one line: the Halloween streaming lift that was last week's news landed in this week's chart — the song climbed to a new Hot 100 peak of No. 3 in its 58th week on that chart, the longest climb to the top three in Hot 100 history — and not one line of the chain behind it moved, while its three crowns all held for another week.

Q&A

Q: What is the No. 1 song on Hot Dance/Electronic Songs this week? A: "Dracula" (remix) by Tame Impala and Jennie, No. 1 on the chart dated November 14, 2026, at 44 total weeks at No. 1 for the combined song entry.

Q: What changed for "Dracula" since the last review in this series? A: The count rose from 43 to 44 total weeks at No. 1 on Hot Dance/Electronic Songs; the song took an eighth consecutive week at No. 1 on Radio Songs (62.6 million audience, reported); it climbed from No. 5 to a new peak of No. 3 on the Hot 100 in its 58th week on that chart, the longest climb to the top three in Hot 100 history (reported); and its tracking week (Oct. 30–Nov. 5) drew a reported 9.4 million U.S. streams, up 31%, and 2,000 sold, up 8%. The credits, the label line, and the team did not change.

Q: What record did "Dracula" set on the Hot 100 this week? A: Reaching the top three in its 58th week on the chart — the longest climb to that region in Hot 100 history, reported by Billboard's Nov. 9 preview, passing the 51 weeks Glass Animals' "Heat Waves" needed in 2021–22. The 58-week count covers the song's whole chart history, including its run before Jennie's version arrived.

Q: Who wrote the "Dracula" remix? A: Kevin Parker and Sarah Aarons wrote the original; the remix adds Jennie and Carly Gibert as writers — unchanged from the first review.

Q: What label released the "Dracula" remix? A: Columbia Records — under exclusive license from Tame Impala, per the recording line itself. Unchanged.

Q: Who worked on the "Dracula" remix behind the scenes? A: Kevin Parker (production, mix, recording), Eric J Dubowsky, Mendy Stein, Jung Eun-kyung, Loren Humphrey, and mastering engineer Matt Colton, plus the lyric video's credited crew — the roll call is above. The publishers and the remix's studios are credited nowhere public found, for a 44th week.
