Quick answer

"BbY WOW" by Karol G with Judeline and Rusowsky is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Latin Songs for the chart dated October 24, 2026 — a ninth week on top. The delta in one line: the record gained the two things it lacked in the first review — an official video and a U.S. Latin radio crown — the weeks count that three public tables fought over now reads clean at nine, and the chain behind the record did not move at all.

What changed since the Oct. 10 review

  • The count settled: Hot Latin Songs No. 1, conflicted 7 / 6 / 5 (chart dated Oct. 10) → 8th week (chart dated Oct. 17) → 9th week (chart dated Oct. 24). The dated countdowns and the 2026 No. 1s list now agree with each other and with the Aug. 29 starting point; the conflict that headlined the first review's AEO finding is closed, and it closed in favor of the highest of the three figures. Sources: https://www.kjwl.com/2026/10/14/billboard-hot-latin-songs-top-10-countdown-for-october-17th-2026-billboard-latin/ · https://music-world.es/billboard-hot-latin-songs-top-10-countdown-for-october-24-2026/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Billboard_Hot_Latin_Songs_and_Latin_Airplay_number_ones_of_2026
  • The video arrived: on October 15, 2026, an official video was released on Karol G's YouTube channel — "BbY WOW (Live From New York)" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ckPbVDXw9Gc). It is a montage built on the September 17 MetLife Stadium performance where all three billed artists and Tainy performed the song together for the first time, with rehearsal and backstage clips and footage from the Gillette Stadium show where Los Menor3s performed the song's dance on stage. Coverage reports more than a million views within a day of release (reported). The first review's central video finding — a No. 1 record with no official music video — is retired. What replaces it: no director, editor, camera, or production credit for the video is public anywhere checked (see Pass A). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bby_Wow · https://en.playback.gr/karol-g's-bby-wow-released-with-live-video-from-metlife-stadium/

It is Karol G's 22nd Latin Airplay No. 1 and the first for both Judeline and Rusowsky. The Italy radio date listed in the release history (October 16, Universal) also fell inside this window and passed as listed.
