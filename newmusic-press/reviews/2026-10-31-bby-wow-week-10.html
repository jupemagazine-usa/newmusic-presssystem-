Quick answer

"BbY WOW" by Karol G with Judeline and Rusowsky is No. 1 on Billboard's Hot Latin Songs for the chart dated October 31, 2026 — a tenth week on top. The delta in one line: the Latin crown is the one crown that held — in the same week the song's six-week run atop both global charts ended, its Latin Airplay crown passed to another song from the same album, and it left the Hot 100 top 10 — while the chain behind the record, and every gap in it, did not move at all.
